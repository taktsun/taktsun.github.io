Q&A

What is the required Excel format?

Each data row is one observation. The tool searches the first 20 rows for column names, ignoring spaces, underscores, capitalization and question-type labels in parentheses. Use the dropdowns to map other column names.

  • Session number: a column with “session” in its header is preferred. Otherwise, the tool looks for an unused column containing 1, 2, 3… for each client. If neither is found, observations are numbered in date/time order. The Session dropdown also lets you choose a column or force date/time order. Supplied session numbers must be positive whole numbers and unique within a client; gaps are preserved.
  • Date and time: the observation timestamp; date, datetime and timestamp are also recognized. Timestamps must be unique within a client.
  • Client: who, alias, name, client, person, participant, subject, patient and respondent are recognized, including forms such as client_id and participant_name. Capitalization distinguishes client identifiers.
  • Tasks: what, task, step, component, activity, technique and intervention are recognized, including plurals. Separate task names with commas. Empty or NaN means no tasks.
  • Expected remaining sessions: howmany and how_many are recognized. Values can be nonnegative numbers, including zero or decimals. Empty or NaN means a missing rating; the line breaks at that session.
  • Use calculated values in place of formulas. Extra columns, including device metadata, are ignored.

If several columns match equally, choose the intended column in the dropdown. Sequence detection requires a complete 1-to-N sequence for every client and at least one client with two observations.

Tasks that appear earlier are shown higher. When tasks start together, the one whose first uninterrupted run ends earlier is shown first.

Clients can have different numbers of observations and tasks. The original timestamps appear in the data table and marker tooltips. Limits: 10 MB per file, 10,000 observations per worksheet and 100 distinct tasks per client. Large graphs scroll within their frame.

Are my data private?

Your workbook and data stays in this browser. Reading, calculations, and drawing are done locally on your device. You can test this by going offline before selecting your Excel file.
